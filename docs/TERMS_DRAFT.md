**Status: internal draft only. This is not a legal agreement. Obtain operator and legal review before publishing or accepting users.**

## Beta availability

PyLake Student Beta v0.1 is not yet cleared for release. If a beta is later offered, it should be described as pre-release software that may change, be unavailable, or lose data. No service-level or support commitment is established by this repository.

## Safe use

Until the operator publishes approved terms and a privacy notice:

- Do not upload personal, confidential, regulated, or production data.
- Use only data you are authorized to process.
- Keep independent copies of any data you cannot afford to lose.
- Do not share passwords, cookies, database URLs, or storage credentials.
- Do not expose the local-development Compose ports to untrusted networks.

The operator must define account eligibility, acceptable use, data ownership and processing rights, service availability, security reporting, termination, data export/deletion, liability, governing law, and contact details. No legal rights or obligations should be inferred from this draft.
